Features

Built for real
emergencies

Every feature is designed for speed. When someone needs blood, every second matters.

Verified Donors Only

Every donor goes through admin document review before they appear in search. No fakes, no unreliable matches.

Voice Search

Say "Find me an O positive donor in Santa Rosa" and the system filters instantly — hands-free for emergencies.

Works Like an App

Install BloodLink on your phone's home screen. Push notifications for requests, even when the browser is closed.

56-Day Cooldown Tracker

Donors are automatically marked unavailable after a donation and return to active status exactly when eligible.

Contact Info Protected

Donor contact details are only revealed to a recipient once the donor accepts their request. Privacy first.

Offline Donor Card

Once verified, your blood type card is cached offline so you can show it at a hospital even without internet.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What data we collect
  • Full name, username, and email address
  • Phone number and location
  • Blood type and donor profile information
  • Uploaded documents (Government ID, Medical Certificate)
  • Donation history and cooldown status
  • Profile picture
How we use your data
  • To match blood donors with recipients in need
  • To verify donor eligibility through document review
  • To send notifications about application status and donation requests
  • To track donation cooldown periods for donor safety
How we store your data

Your data is stored securely in our database. Uploaded documents are stored on our servers and are only accessible to authorized administrators for verification purposes.

Who can see your data
  • Your username, location, and blood type are visible to recipients when browsing donors
  • Your uploaded documents are only visible to admins during verification
  • Your full name, profile picture, and contact details are only shared when a donation request is accepted
Your rights

You may contact the BloodLink admin team for any concerns regarding your personal data. Please note that admin capabilities are limited to reviewing and approving or rejecting donor applications and viewing user profiles. Data modification or account removal is not currently supported.
