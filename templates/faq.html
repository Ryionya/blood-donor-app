🩸 FAQ & How It Works

Everything you need to know about using BloodLink

📖 How BloodLink Works
For Donors
  1. Register and complete your profile — full name, profile picture, location, and contact number.
  2. Apply as a donor by uploading a valid government ID and a medical certificate or blood typing result.
  3. Wait for admin review. You'll receive a notification once your application is approved or rejected.
  4. Once verified, your donor card becomes visible to recipients and you receive a verified badge.
  5. Toggle your availability anytime from your dashboard. Unavailable donors don't appear in search results.
  6. When a recipient sends you a request, you'll be notified. You can accept or decline.
  7. After accepting, both you and the recipient can see each other's contact info to coordinate directly.
  8. After donating, log it in the system. A 56-day cooldown begins automatically.
For Recipients
  1. Register and complete your profile — full name, blood type, location, and a valid government ID.
  2. Browse verified available donors filtered by blood type compatible with yours and by location.
  3. Click on a donor card to view their profile.
  4. Send a donation request — fill in the hospital or clinic name where the transfusion will take place, the date needed, blood bags needed, a message to the donor, and your medical certificate.
  5. Your request is reviewed by the admin before being sent to the donor.
  6. Once the donor accepts, contact information is revealed on both sides so you can coordinate directly.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions

After registering, complete your profile with your full name, profile picture, location, and contact number. Then go to Apply as Donor and upload a valid government ID and a medical certificate or blood typing result. An admin will review your documents — usually within 1–2 days. You'll receive a notification once a decision is made. If approved, you'll get a verified badge and become discoverable by recipients.

Go to Browse Donors. The list automatically shows donors with blood types compatible with yours. You can filter further by location. Click on a donor card to view their profile, then click Request Donation. Fill in the hospital or clinic name where the transfusion will take place (not your home address), the date you need the blood, how many blood bags are needed, a message to the donor, and attach your medical certificate. Once your request is reviewed and the donor accepts, contact information is revealed on both sides.

After every whole blood donation, donors must wait at least 56 days (8 weeks) before donating again. This is a standard medical guideline to allow the body to fully replenish red blood cells. During this period, the donor's status is automatically set to Unavailable and they won't appear in search results. Once the cooldown ends, availability is restored automatically. Donors can track their cooldown from their dashboard.

For donors: A valid government-issued ID (e.g. PhilSys, driver's license, passport, UMID) and a medical certificate or blood typing result confirming your blood type.

For recipients per request: A valid government ID on your profile, and a medical certificate confirming the need for blood transfusion attached to each donation request.

These documents are used strictly for verification purposes and are handled with strict confidentiality. They are reviewed only by BloodLink admins and are never shared with other users.

When you submit your donor application, an admin reviews your uploaded documents to confirm your identity and blood type. They check that the face on the ID matches your profile, that the blood type on your medical certificate matches what you entered, and that all details are consistent. If approved, you receive a verified badge and your profile becomes visible to recipients. If rejected, you'll be notified with a reason and can re-submit with corrected documents.

Contact information is only revealed after the donor accepts your request. This protects the privacy of all users. Once accepted, both the donor and recipient can see each other's contact details on the request page to coordinate directly.

Verified donors have a digital donor card that displays their blood type, verified badge, and basic profile information. Recipients can view the donor card on the donor's profile page. The donor card can also be downloaded or printed and shown at a hospital even without internet access, thanks to offline caching on the BloodLink app.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What data we collect
  • Full name, username, and email address
  • Phone number and location
  • Blood type and donor profile information
  • Uploaded documents (Government ID, Medical Certificate)
  • Donation history and cooldown status
  • Profile picture (optional)
How we use your data
  • To match blood donors with recipients in need
  • To verify donor eligibility through document review
  • To send notifications about application status and donation requests
  • To track donation cooldown periods for donor safety
How we store your data

Your data is stored securely in our database. Uploaded documents are stored on our servers and are only accessible to authorized administrators for verification purposes.

Who can see your data
  • Your name and blood type are visible to recipients when browsing donors
  • Your uploaded documents are only visible to admins during verification
  • Your contact details are only shared when a donation request is accepted
Your rights

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting the BloodLink admin team.
